01  Record

It is recording before you are awake.

Add a streamer once. When they go live, Peaksnip starts recording by itself and keeps the last stretch of stream, checking it as often as every minute.

02  Find

Two judges, one verdict.

One AI reads only what was said. Another hears only how loud it got and how busy chat was. A moment is kept when both agree, so you get reactions, not filler.

03  Cut

Each moment becomes a vertical clip.

Cut on a finished sentence, framed for 9:16, with word-by-word captions and a hook line on top. A title, description and hashtags come with it.

04  Post

Straight to Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Connect your accounts once, then queue or auto-post with spacing and daily caps. Or download the files and post them yourself.

Specs

What it does, plainly.

No plans and no limits unless you set them. You bring the AI key and the machine; everything else is in the box.

Clips from
YouTube, Twitch and Kick links, live or recorded · or your own video files

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Peaksnip free?

Yes. Peaksnip is free and open source. You run it on your own computer or server, and there are no plans or fees.

Which AI does it use?

Your own. Add an API key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude or OpenRouter, or point it at a local model. Whatever the AI costs is between you and that provider.

What happens without an AI key?

Peaksnip still makes clips, picking the loudest moments that have speech in them. Add a key and the AI chooses the moments and writes the hooks and titles.

Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Links can be from YouTube, Twitch or Kick, or you can upload a video file. Captions are written from English speech.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Vertical 9:16, the shape Shorts, Reels and TikTok use. They run 15 to 60 seconds by default and you can set your own range. Choose a blurred fill, a zoomed frame or a centre crop.

Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip to trim the start and end, rewrite the hook and title, or switch caption style and layout, then re-make it.

Can I clip any streamer?

Peaksnip is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
